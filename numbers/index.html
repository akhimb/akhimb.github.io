About Number Playground

Number Playground is a toddler-centric educational Progressive Web App designed to build early numeracy skills through multi-sensory learning:

  • ASMR Chalkboard Writing: Realistic chalk physics, gentle tactile vibration, and soothing scratchy chalk sound effects teach handwriting without stress or punishment.
  • Interactive Counting: Replaces rigid checkboxes with joyful, tactile tap-and-bounce objects and big colorful choice cards.
  • Real Spoken Voice: Natural spoken pronunciation for numbers, animals, and questions keeps toddlers engaged and learning correct pronunciation.
  • Surprise Toys: Rewarding positive reinforcement to celebrate progress!
  • Installable & Offline: Play anywhere without needing internet.
